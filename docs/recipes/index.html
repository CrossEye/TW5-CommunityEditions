Tidbits

A TiddlyWiki edition for storing, browsing, and cooking from your own recipes.

Try it

  • Open the populated edition — comes with 32 sample recipes and ingredients to browse.
  • Open the empty edition — nothing but the structure; populate it yourself or drop in a sample collection.

Sample collections

Browse downloadable sample collections — load a collection into the empty edition by dragging the JSON file onto the wiki.

About

Tidbits is a community edition: an empty TiddlyWiki tuned for one purpose, with documentation, sample data, and a reset-to-empty affordance baked in. The full source lives at github.com/CrossEye/TW5-CommunityEditions.

This is a temporary host. A dedicated TiddlyWiki community editions site is planned; URLs will move when it ships.
